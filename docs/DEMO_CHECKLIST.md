# SAHELI — College Presentation & Demo Checklist

**Product:** SAHELI — Smart Women Safety & Emergency Assistance Platform
**Tagline:** *"Safety before the emergency."*
**Core Model:** $\text{PREVENT} \to \text{ASSIST} \to \text{ESCALATE} \to \text{RECOVER}$
**Simulation Notice:** *All emergency actions are simulations. No real calls, messages, location sharing, or emergency services are triggered.*

---

## Live Demonstration Walkthrough

Follow this step-by-step sequence during the project evaluation:

### Part 1: Proactive SafeWalk & Escalation Flow
1. **Open SAHELI** in the browser (`http://localhost:5173/`).
2. **Show Landing Banner** on the Dashboard: highlight the women-safety mission, the 4-phase continuum, and prototype transparency badge.
3. **Open Dashboard**: review the baseline `SAFE` status, mock battery and availability of Primary Guardian (*Mother*), and nearest verified safe place (*Apollo 24/7 Pharmacy*).
4. **Start SafeWalk**: click **"Start SafeWalk"** or navigate to the SafeWalk tab; review the companion route setup (Campus to Home, ETA 7:30 PM, 4-minute check-in).
5. **Click "I'm Uncomfortable"**: demonstrate proactive early intervention before danger occurs.
6. **Show CAUTION Mode**: amber alert treatment with de-escalation suite and quick safe haven directions.
7. **Show Safe Place**: click **"View Details"** on Apollo 24/7 Pharmacy; explain why recommended (24/7 staff, well-lit entrance, CCTV).
8. **Alert Guardian (simulation)**: trigger **"Alert Mother"** standby notification; observe toast confirmation and Incident Timeline entry.
9. **Escalate to THREAT**: click **"Escalate Situation"**; interface turns orange, prioritizing immediate shelter or emergency action.
10. **Activate Emergency**: click **"SOS Emergency"**; full-screen high-priority alert activates.
11. **Show Simulated Response**: observe standby broadcast logs, verified safe haven suggestions, and forensic timestamp ledger.
12. **Mark User Safe**: click **"I'm Safe (Resolve Emergency)"**.
13. **Show RECOVERED → SAFE**: view reassurance state and automatic normalization back to `SAFE` status.

---

### Part 2: Interactive Scenario Engine (Safety Simulator)
14. **Open Safety Simulator**: click **"Simulator"** in the top navigation or via the quick banner.
15. **Run "BEING FOLLOWED"**: select the scenario marked with the `RECOMMENDED DEMO` badge; click **"RUN SCENARIO"**.
16. **Use NEXT STEP to Demonstrate Escalation**:
    - Click **[ NEXT STEP ]** one step at a time so the evaluators can observe how SAHELI responds at each inflection point:
      - Step 1: Journey Started (`SAFE`)
      - Step 2: User reports discomfort (`CAUTION`)
      - Step 3: CAUTION MODE (`CAUTION`)
      - Step 4: Safe place recommended (`CAUTION`)
      - Step 5: Guardian alert simulated (`CAUTION`)
      - Step 6: THREAT MODE (`THREAT`)
      - Step 7: Emergency mode (`EMERGENCY`)
      - Step 8: User marked safe (`RECOVERED`)
      - Step 9: RECOVERED (`RECOVERED`)
      - Step 10: SAFE (`SAFE`)
17. **Show Final SAFE State & Completion Screen**:
    - Observe `SIMULATION COMPLETE`: *"SAHELI successfully guided the user from risk detection to recovery."*
    - Show `[ RUN AGAIN ]` and `[ CHOOSE ANOTHER SCENARIO ]` options.

*Note: All actions and notifications shown are client-side simulated demonstrations.*
